Fundamentals of Digital Marketing

What digital marketing is (and isn't), its four objectives, why people go online, the seven key components (website, SEO/SEM, social, paid ads, remarketing, affiliate, email), Kaushik's measurement model, and metrics vs KPIs.

1What is digital marketing?

Digital வழியாக வாடிக்கையாளரை அணுகுதல்

Internet மற்றும் digital channels மூலம் value-ஐத் தெரிவித்து customer relationship உருவாக்குவது. Social media post மட்டும் அல்ல; website, search, email, mobile, measurement அனைத்தும் சேரலாம்.

மேலும் புரிந்துகொள்ள · தேர்வுக் குறிப்புகள்

பாடத்தைப் படிப்படியாகப் புரிந்துகொள்ள

Digital marketing digital technologies/channels மூலம் customer value/relationship/outcome உருவாக்குகிறது. Course online channels-ஐ முக்கியமாகக் கையாள்கிறது; digital TV/display போன்றவற்றை “digital அல்ல” என்று முழுவதும் நீக்க முடியாது. Instant feedback இருப்பது மட்டும் definition அல்ல. Targeting/measurement useful; எல்லா actions-உம் perfect-ஆக track ஆகாது. Consent choices, device changes, attribution limits காரணமாக uncertainty இருக்கும்.

The course describes the expansion from traditional campaigns to websites, apps, search and social platforms. Statements such as 'the last ten years' are historical rhetoric, not a dated quantitative comparison.

Definition

Digital marketing is the promotion of products or brands via one or more forms of electronic media. The course mainly covers online/internet marketing; digital marketing can also include digital channels outside the web. More precisely, it is the strategy and processes that connect advertisers with audiences across digital channels.

Course focus Traditional forms contrasted with it
Banner ads, SEO, pay-per-click, email, RSS, voice/fax broadcast, blogging, podcasting, video streams, SMS, instant messaging Conventional print and non-digital broadcast/outdoor formats; digital TV, streaming audio and digital displays blur this boundary. Instant feedback is not the defining test

Key ideas:

  • Digital inventory is the space a publisher makes available for ads on its platform.
  • Channels: display, search, mobile, social, video, each serving different stages of the conversion funnel.
  • "Inbound" and interactive: audiences can interact with ads and respond to advertisers.
  • Precise targeting: by demographic, behavioural and psychographic attributes, by device, even by individual user, without losing scale.
  • Measurable: impressions, clicks, leads and purchases can be tracked with appropriate setup, but consent choices, devices and attribution limit completeness. Attributed revenue is not automatically incremental profit.

2Objectives of digital marketing

Digital நோக்கங்கள்

Awareness, leads, sales, retention போன்றவற்றில் எது இலக்கு என்று முதலில் சொல்லுங்கள். Followers அதிகரித்ததால் மட்டும் வியாபாரம் வளர்ந்தது என்று சொல்ல முடியாது. நோக்கத்துடன் பொருந்தும் metric தேர்வு செய்ய வேண்டும்.

மேலும் புரிந்துகொள்ள · தேர்வுக் குறிப்புகள்

பாடத்தைப் படிப்படியாகப் புரிந்துகொள்ள

நான்கு objectives: online sales, qualified leads, acquisition cost குறைத்தல், engagement/retention. Car dealer-க்கு lead→offline sale; online store-க்கு purchase நேரடியாக இருக்கலாம். Revenue/ad spend என்பது ROAS; profit return வேறு. Digital always cheaper அல்ல; audience, competition, creative, conversion ஆகியவை cost-ஐ மாற்றும்.

Objectives differ by business:

  • An online apparel store sells completely online.
  • A car dealer uses online as a first contact point; the purchase happens offline.
Objective Explanation Example
1. Increase direct sales Campaigns bring visitors who buy online, so revenue per advertising dollar (ROAS, not profit ROI) can be measured. Not every campaign sells directly (there are assisted conversions and awareness too) An online dry-fruit store runs Facebook ads; customers pay online and get courier delivery
2. Continuous flow of new leads For offline businesses, generate qualified leads online for sales staff to convert. It also increases walk-ins A car dealer runs search, social and email ads to gather leads
3. Reduce customer-acquisition cost Aim to reach a relevant audience efficiently; actual acquisition cost depends on competition, creative, conversion and measurement An FMCG firm targets one town with social or search ads instead of newspaper ads, billboards and kiosks
4. Keep customers engaged Acquisition alone doesn't bring repeat buys. Stay connected through social media and retargeting An apparel store shows a browser the exact items they viewed

3Why people go online

மக்கள் Online ஏன்?

தகவல், தொடர்பு, பொழுதுபோக்கு, வசதி, shopping போன்ற வெவ்வேறு நோக்கங்கள் உண்டு. செய்தி வாசிக்க வந்தவருக்கு உடனடி விற்பனை அழுத்தம் பொருந்தாமல் இருக்கலாம். User intent புரிய வேண்டும்.

மேலும் புரிந்துகொள்ள · தேர்வுக் குறிப்புகள்

பாடத்தைப் படிப்படியாகப் புரிந்துகொள்ள

ஒன்பது intents: information, communication, entertainment, work, school, relationships, market/buy-sell, self-expression, help. Search query-இன் intent-க்கு content பொருந்த வேண்டும்; தகவல் தேடும் எல்லோரிடமும் உடனடி purchase எதிர்பார்க்க வேண்டாம்.

Nine reasons, from research:

Reason Meaning
1. Information The largest library ever: text, images, video, audio
2. Communication Email, voice and video calls: cheaper and faster relationships
3. Entertainment Games, virtual worlds, music, video (watch for addiction)
4. Work Telecommuting; earning a living online (IT, web, internet marketing)
5. School Curricula from primary school to university require the internet
6. Relationships Find, maintain or end relationships; social networks
7. Market Research, find, buy and sell, 24/7
8. Self-expression People create content: politically, artistically, socially
9. Ask for help Privacy and anonymity: emotional support, medical advice
Marketing implication

Each reason is a touchpoint: informative content, communities, entertaining videos, e-commerce, user-generated content, and help or support forums.

4Key components of a digital marketing strategy

Digital strategy பகுதிகள்

Target audience, value proposition, content, channels, customer journey, budget, measurement ஒன்றாகத் திட்டமிட வேண்டும். ஒரு channel பிரபலமாக இருப்பதால் மட்டும் எல்லா நிறுவனமும் அதிலேயே செலவிட வேண்டியதில்லை.

மேலும் புரிந்துகொள்ள · தேர்வுக் குறிப்புகள்

ஏழு Components: company website; SEO/search marketing; social media; paid/PPC ads; remarketing; affiliate; email. SEO unpaid/organic visibility மேம்பாடு; PPC ad click-க்கு செலவு; remarketing முன்பு தொடர்புடைய audience-ஐ மீண்டும் அணுகுதல்; affiliate referral/result அடிப்படையிலான partner promotion. Website fast, secure, mobile-friendly, clear navigation, contact தகவலுடன் இருக்க வேண்டும்.

பாடத்தைப் படிப்படியாகப் புரிந்துகொள்ள

ஏழு components: website; search/SEO; social; paid ads; remarketing; affiliates; email. Website fast/HTTPS/mobile-friendly/readable/actionable இருக்கட்டும்; contact top-right மட்டுமே விதி அல்ல. 53%/3-second figure 2016 study; current guarantee இல்லை. SEO organic visibility; paid search ads வேறு. SEM சில இடங்களில் umbrella, சில இடங்களில் paid search மட்டும். PPC per-click billing; எல்லா social/influencer ad-உம் PPC அல்ல. Remarketing cookie மட்டும் அல்ல; permitted audience data பல முறைகளில் இருக்கலாம்; cookie IDs தானாக anonymous அல்ல. Affiliate commission sale/lead போன்ற agreed outcome-க்கு; fraud/refunds/low margins காரணமாக waste இல்லாதது அல்ல. Email opt-in, useful content, unsubscribe ஆகியவை நம்பிக்கைக்கு உதவும். 1. Company website: "your storefront"

The website is the mother of all online real estate and your best marketing asset. Driving leads and sales through company-created content is inbound marketing. A successful site must be:

Must be Why
Fast Fast loading supports usability. The 53% abandonment beyond 3 seconds figure comes from a 2016 Google mobile-web sample, not a universal current threshold (study)
Secure (HTTPS) Consumers know about hacks; any form collecting personal data must be secure; security is a search-ranking factor
Mobile-friendly Design for the actual audience and devices; mobile-friendly pages matter. The course's traffic/voice-search trends are not current universal measurements
Clean, easy design with contact info Contact details in an easy-to-find, task-appropriate location; clear calls-to-action, tappable navigation: good UX
SEO-optimised Search is one discovery route alongside referrals, direct visits, social media and other channels

2. Search engine optimisation and marketing

Improve search engines' understanding and users' discovery of relevant content; top rankings are not guaranteed for keywords, e.g. "computer repair shops in San Francisco".

  • On-page SEO optimises your web pages.
  • Off-page SEO includes earning relevant links and reputation; social promotion can aid discovery, but do not assume social likes are a guaranteed direct ranking signal.
  • SEO takes time: rankings don't change overnight. That's why it may not be effective in the short run.
Note on terms

The PDF uses SEM and SEO as if they were the same. Strictly:

  • SEM is used as an umbrella for SEO + paid search in some texts; many practitioners use it for paid search only. Define the convention before comparing terms.
  • SEO is the unpaid part.

3. Social media marketing

Social platforms support content, community and advertising. The course's 7-in-10 and 17% claims are dated illustrations without a verified current sampling basis; do not present them as universal population rates.

  • Basic management is easy (profiles, posting, engaging).
  • Paid social ads need expertise, but allow targeting by geography, age, gender, interests.

4. Paid advertising (pay-per-click)

The quickest way to reach a target audience:

  1. Make the ad.
  2. Pick the audience (location, demographics, interests).
  3. Set a budget.

PPC means you pay only when someone clicks. The main platform is Google Ads (AdWords): search pages, partner sites, YouTube. Other platforms offer multiple billing models. Not all social, influencer or blogger promotions are PPC; they may charge for impressions, actions or a fixed placement.

5. Remarketing (retargeting)

"Ever feel like websites you visited are following you with ads?"

  1. With appropriate permissions and disclosures, an advertiser may build audiences from site/app interactions or customer data. Cookie identifiers are not automatically anonymous; tracking is subject to consent, privacy choices and platform restrictions.
  2. If the audience is eligible and an ad opportunity is available, the platform may show a relevant ad. Delivery is not guaranteed; methods are not limited to third-party cookies.

The course quotes a 70% conversion-lift claim without a study design here. Treat it as historical promotional context, not a universal or causal guarantee.

6. Affiliate marketing

Third-party affiliates promote your product for a commission: a % of revenue or a fixed fee per sale.

  • Payment is based on an agreed action, often a sale or lead. Commission, refunds, fraud and programme costs mean there can still be wasted spend.
  • Keys to success: a great product, lucrative payouts, the right affiliates.

7. Email marketing

Direct emails to prospects and customers, run in-house or by an agency.

  • Bought or cold lists perform poorly (people didn't opt in).
  • The challenge: reaching the inbox, not spam. Use strong creative content and subject lines for a high open rate.

There's more: online PR, content marketing and others.

5Strategy & measurement

Strategy மற்றும் Measurement

Reach கவனத்திற்கு வந்ததைக் காட்டும்; conversion தேவையான செயல் நடந்ததைக் காட்டும். Cost per acquisition, retention போன்றவை business outcome-உடன் இணைகின்றன. ஒரு metric மட்டும் பார்த்து optimisation செய்யாதீர்கள்.

Conversion rate = desired actions/பொருத்தமான visitors அல்லது opportunities×100; denominator வரையறுக்கப்பட வேண்டும். Acquisition cost = relevant campaign spend/new acquired customers. Likes-ஐ actual purchases என்று மாற்றிப் பொருள் கொள்ளாதீர்கள்.

பாடத்தைப் படிப்படியாகப் புரிந்துகொள்ள

Default demo: spend100,000; impressions200,000; clicks4,000; leads400; sales60; revenue450,000. CTR2%; CPC25; CPL250; lead→sale15%; cost/sale1,666.67; ROAS4.5×. Margin40% என்ற கற்பனை assumption-இல் contribution180,000; marketing return=(180,000−100,000)/100,000=80%. பழைய350% figure costs-ஐ விட்ட revenue-minus-spend; profit ROMI அல்ல. ROAS attribution தானாக incremental effect நிரூபிக்காது. Zero denominator-க்கு “—”; எல்லா sales-உம் புதிய customers அல்ல என்பதால் cost/sale மற்றும் acquisition cost வேறுபடலாம்.

Start with a digital competitive analysis. Compare your online presence with top competitors to see where you stand and which opportunities they're taking that you aren't. Then build a Digital Marketing & Measurement Model and a roadmap of priority channels.

Why digital campaigns fail

It's usually not a lack of creativity or a less attractive website. It's a lack of structured thinking about the campaign's real purpose and no objective measures of success.

Kaushik's 5-step Digital Marketing & Measurement Model

Step What Who leads
1. Business objectives Identify objectives up front; the broadest parameters Senior executives
2. Goals Crisp goals for each objective Executives lead; you contribute
3. KPIs Write down the key performance indicators You (with a data person)
4. Targets Success targets for each KPI, set up front Organisation leaders, with Marketing and Finance input
5. Segments The people, behaviours and outcomes to analyse to understand why you succeeded or failed Analysts

Metric vs KPI

Metric = a number

  • Bounce rate
  • Page views
  • Time on page
  • New visitors
  • Search rankings

Useful for specific tactics.

KPI = a metric closely tied to overall business success

  • Total sales, revenue, leads
  • ROMI (return on marketing investment)
  • Lead-to-sale conversion rate
  • Booking rate (from calls)
  • Cost per lead (CPL)

Three KPIs for a tourist hotel's digital campaign
  1. Online booking revenue (or booking rate) from the campaign
  2. Cost per booking (or per lead)
  3. ROMI

Supporting metrics: website visits from ads, bounce rate on the booking page.

Strategy மற்றும் Measurement

Reach கவனத்திற்கு வந்ததைக் காட்டும்; conversion தேவையான செயல் நடந்ததைக் காட்டும். Cost per acquisition, retention போன்றவை business outcome-உடன் இணைகின்றன. ஒரு metric மட்டும் பார்த்து optimisation செய்யாதீர்கள்.

Kaushik 5 steps: business objectives → goals → KPIs → targets → segments. Metric அளவு; KPI இலக்கிற்கு முக்கியமாகத் தேர்ந்த அளவு. எல்லா metrics-உம் KPI அல்ல. Conversion rate = desired actions/பொருத்தமான visitors அல்லது opportunities×100; denominator வரையறுக்கப்பட வேண்டும். Acquisition cost = relevant campaign spend/new acquired customers. Likes-ஐ actual purchases என்று மாற்றிப் பொருள் கொள்ளாதீர்கள்.
