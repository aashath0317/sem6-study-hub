## What is overhead?

:::key Definition
**Overhead** is the **total of indirect materials, indirect labour and indirect expenses**: costs incurred in making a product, providing a service or running a department that **can't be traced directly and in full** to the product [[01:43]].
:::

:::example The biscuit company [[02:20]]
- **Direct:** we can measure the **flour** and the **labour hours** per packet of biscuits.
- **Indirect (overhead):** we **can't** say how much **electricity, water, or the production manager's salary** went into one packet.
:::

| Classification by element [[03:42]] | Classification by function |
|---|---|
| **Indirect materials** (cleaning materials, lubricants) | **Production overhead**: factory electricity |
| **Indirect labour** (supervisors, storekeepers) | **Selling and distribution overhead**: showroom electricity |
| **Indirect expenses** (rent, insurance, depreciation) | **Administration overhead**: HR building electricity |

Overheads can't be traced to a unit directly, so they reach each unit through **three stages**:

:::flow
1. Allocation
2. Apportionment (+ re-apportionment of service depts)
3. Absorption into units
:::

## Cost centres, allocation & apportionment

A **cost centre** is a **responsibility centre** whose manager is accountable for the expenses under their control. In simple terms, a **department** [[06:05]].

Cost centres can be **production**, **service** or **process** cost centres.

| | **Allocation** | **Apportionment** |
|---|---|---|
| **Meaning** | **Whole** cost items charged **directly** to one cost centre | **Sharing** a common cost **in proportions** between two or more cost centres on a **fair basis** |
| **Example** | Each department has its **own electricity meter**. Food colouring used only by Dept A (Rs 15,000) | **One** electricity bill or the factory **rent** shared by floor area |

### Bases of apportionment

| Overhead | Basis |
|---|---|
| Rent, rates and taxes, lighting and heating, building insurance, depreciation and repairs, air conditioning | **Floor area occupied** |
| Insurance and depreciation of plant and machinery | **Capital value** (of machines) |
| Repairs and maintenance of plant and machinery | **Machine hours** |
| Welfare, time-keeping, payroll office, canteen, recreation, supervision, medical | **Number of employees** |
| Store-keeping expenses | **Value or weight of materials** (or number of requisitions) |
| Transport service | **Truck mileage, tonnage, truck hours, number of packages** |
| Electricity (power) | **Kilowatt usage** (or floor area) [[11:51]] |

:::example Video apportionment example [[12:32]]
- **Electricity Rs 225,000** by kW usage 500 : 400 : 100 = **5 : 4 : 1** (A : B : maintenance): A = 225,000 ÷ 10 × 5 = **112,500**, B = 90,000, maintenance = 22,500 [[15:01]].
- **Rent Rs 150,000** by floor area 2,500 : 3,750 : 1,250 m² = **2 : 3 : 1**: A = 150,000 ÷ 6 × 2 = **50,000**, B = 75,000, maintenance = 25,000 [[16:01]].
- **Machine insurance Rs 45,000** by **machine value** 300k : 500k : 100k = **3 : 5 : 1**: A 15,000, B 25,000, maintenance 5,000 [[17:08]].
- **Machine depreciation Rs 72,000**, also 3 : 5 : 1: A 24,000, B 40,000, maintenance 8,000.

Adding the directly allocated items (A 15,000 · B 60,000 · maintenance 25,000), the **primary totals** are **A 216,500 · B 290,000 · maintenance 85,500** [[17:35]].

**Re-apportionment:** maintenance 85,500 by maintenance hours 3,000 : 2,000 (3 : 2) gives A **+51,300** and B **+34,200**. **Final: A 267,800 · B 324,200** [[19:28]].
:::

## Re-apportioning service department costs

- **Production departments** carry out the **central purpose** of the organisation.
- **Service departments** (stores, maintenance, canteen, personnel, accounting) **support** them and have **no output of their own**.
- So their costs must be **re-apportioned to production departments** before absorption [[18:14]].

| Service cost centre | Re-apportionment basis |
|---|---|
| **Stores** | **Number (or value) of material requisitions** |
| **Maintenance** | **Hours of maintenance work** for each centre |
| **Purchasing** | Number of purchase orders, or value of materials purchased |
| **Inspection** | Inspection hours |
| **Internal transport** | Weight and distance |

**Three methods:**

| Method | When |
|---|---|
| **1. Direct method** | **Ignore services between service departments**. Apportion each service department **straight to production departments** |
| **2. Step-down method** | Some service departments serve others, but the services **aren't reciprocated**. Apportion one service department, including its share to the others, then the next |
| **3. Reciprocal method** | Service departments **serve each other**. Use **repeated distribution** or **simultaneous equations** (let S = stores total, M = maintenance total…) |

:::example Lecture example: direct method
| | Prod A | Prod B | Stores | Maintenance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Overheads | 10,030 | 8,970 | 10,000 | 8,000 |
| Material requisitions (Rs) | 30,000 | 50,000 | – | – |
| Maintenance hours | 8,000 | 1,000 | – | – |

- **Stores (10,000)** by requisitions 30 : 50: A = 10,000 × 3/8 = **3,750**, B = **6,250**
- **Maintenance (8,000)** by hours 8,000 : 1,000: A = 8,000 × 8/9 = **7,111**, B = **889**

| | Prod A | Prod B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Own overheads | 10,030 | 8,970 |
| From stores | 3,750 | 6,250 |
| From maintenance | 7,111 | 889 |
| **Total** | **20,891** | **16,109** |

**Check:** 20,891 + 16,109 = 37,000, the total of all four departments.
:::

## Overhead absorption

:::key Definition
**Absorption** is the systematic process of **charging the overheads of production departments to the units produced**, using an **overhead absorption rate (OAR)** [[21:44]].
:::

:::formula
**Actual OAR = Actual overheads ÷ Actual activity**
**Predetermined OAR = Budgeted overheads ÷ Budgeted activity**  ← normally used
:::

**Why predetermined?** Actual figures are only known at the **end of the period**. Product costs are needed **now**, for **pricing, planning and control** [[28:19]].

### Methods of absorption

| Method | OAR formula | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Production unit** | POH ÷ number of units | Identical products (every biscuit packet is the same) [[24:27]] |
| **% of direct labour cost** | POH ÷ direct labour cost × 100 | |
| **% of direct material cost** | POH ÷ direct material cost × 100 | |
| **% of prime cost** | POH ÷ prime cost × 100 | |
| **Direct labour hour rate** | POH ÷ labour hours | **Labour-intensive** departments [[23:52]] |
| **Machine hour rate** | POH ÷ machine hours | **Machine-intensive** departments |

- **Blanket (single) rate:** **one rate for the whole factory**. Simple, but inaccurate when departments differ.
- **Departmental rates:** a **separate OAR for each production department**. More accurate.

:::example Video absorption example [[25:46]]
- **Dept A** (labour-intensive): OH 267,800 ÷ 4,000 labour hours = **Rs 66.95 per labour hour**. A packet needing 2 labour hours absorbs **Rs 133.90**.
- **Dept B** (machine-intensive): OH 324,200 ÷ 2,000 machine hours = **Rs 162.10 per machine hour** [[26:55]].
:::

## Under- and over-absorption

**Absorbed overhead** = predetermined OAR × **actual** activity. Compare it with **actual overhead**:

| Result | Meaning | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Over-absorption** | **Absorbed > actual**: costs were overstated [[29:03]] | **Credit** to the P&L (adds to profit) |
| **Under-absorption** | **Absorbed < actual**: costs were understated [[29:24]] | **Debit** to the P&L (an extra expense) |

**Causes:**

- **actual overheads differ** from budget
- **actual activity differs** from budget
- **both**

:::exam
A typical question is a **full overhead analysis sheet**:

1. **Allocate** direct items.
2. **Apportion** shared costs with the **right bases** (state them).
3. **Re-apportion** the service departments (direct, step-down or reciprocal).
4. Compute **departmental OARs** (labour hour vs machine hour, and say why).
5. **Cost a unit or job.**
6. Work out the **under/over absorption**.

Also define allocation vs apportionment and explain why predetermined rates are used.
:::

## Quick revision sheet

| Concept | Remember it as |
|---|---|
| Overhead | Indirect material + indirect labour + indirect expenses |
| Function classes | Production · selling & distribution · administration |
| Cost centre | Department; production, service or process |
| Allocation | Whole cost to one centre |
| Apportionment | Share on a fair basis (floor area, capital value, employees, machine hours, kW) |
| Re-apportionment | Service → production: direct · step-down · reciprocal (repeated distribution, equations) |
| Direct-method example | A 20,891 · B 16,109 (total 37,000) |
| Absorption | Charge to units with an OAR |
| OAR | Budgeted OH ÷ budgeted activity (predetermined) |
| Methods | Unit · % labour, material or prime cost · labour hour · machine hour |
| Blanket vs departmental | One rate vs one per department (more accurate) |
| Video example | 267,800 ÷ 4,000 = 66.95/hr; × 2 hrs = 133.90 per unit |
| Over-absorbed | Absorbed > actual → credit P&L |
| Under-absorbed | Absorbed < actual → debit P&L |
