## Week 01 — Introduction to Marketing

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** Marketing என்பது விற்பது (selling) மட்டும் அல்ல. வாடிக்கையாளரின் **தேவையைக் கண்டுபிடித்து**, அதை **லாபத்துடன் நிறைவேற்றுவது** தான் marketing. Selling என்பது iceberg-இன் மேலே தெரியும் சிறு முனை மட்டுமே. அதற்குக் கீழே research, product design, pricing போன்ற பெரிய "homework" மறைந்திருக்கிறது.

**3 definitions (ஒன்றை quote செய்யுங்கள்):**

| Source | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Kotler** | "Meeting needs profitably" |
| **CIM** | The management process responsible for **identifying, anticipating and satisfying customer requirements profitably** |
| **AMA** | The activity, set of institutions and processes for **creating, communicating, delivering and exchanging offerings that have value** for customers, clients, partners and society |

**Marketing vs selling:**

- **Iceberg:** selling and advertising are only the **tip**. Below the water sits the homework: research, product, price, place.
- **Drucker:** "The aim of marketing is to make **selling superfluous**."

**Why marketing matters: three faces**

| Face | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Philosophy | Customer focus across the firm → **effectiveness** |
| Function | Marketing department activities → **efficiency** |
| Skill | **Sensing** the market + **relating** to customers |

**Core concepts:**

- **Need → Want → Demand:** a need is a state of deprivation (hunger). A want is the culturally shaped object (rice and curry). Demand is a want backed by **buying power**.
- **8 demand states:** Negative, Nonexistent, Latent, Declining, Irregular, Full, Overfull, Unwholesome.
- **Customer perceived value** = perceived benefits − perceived sacrifice (cost).
    - **Utilitarian** value = function.
    - **Hedonic** value = prestige (e.g. BMW).
- **Satisfaction:** performance compared with expectations.
    - Below expectations → dissatisfied.
    - Meets them → satisfied.
    - Exceeds them → **delighted**.
- **Exchange conditions:**
    - At least 2 parties.
    - Each has something of value.
    - They can communicate and deliver.
    - Free to accept or reject.
    - Each believes it is appropriate to deal.
- **Markets:** resource, manufacturer, intermediary, consumer and government markets.

**Objectives of marketing:**

- Customer satisfaction
- Creating appropriate demand
- Quality
- Goodwill
- Profitable sales volume

:::exam
**Answer frame: "Distinguish marketing from selling."**

> Selling focuses on the seller's need to convert products into cash, while marketing focuses on the buyer's needs. Using the iceberg analogy, selling and advertising are only the visible tip; beneath lie research, product development, pricing and distribution. As Drucker said, the aim of marketing is to make selling superfluous.
:::

## Week 02 — Paradigms (Philosophies) of Marketing

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** Marketing-இன் சிந்தனை காலப்போக்கில் **6 கட்டங்களாக** வளர்ந்தது. முதலில் "நிறையத் தயாரி" (production), இறுதியில் "எல்லாமே முக்கியம்" (holistic). வரிசையை மனப்பாடம் செய்யுங்கள்: **Production → Product → Selling → Marketing → Societal → Holistic**.

| # | Concept | Belief | Remember |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Production** | Customers want products that are **available and cheap** | Efficiency, economies of scale, Ford Model T |
| 2 | **Product** | Customers want the **best quality and features** | Risk = **marketing myopia** (Levitt). "Better mousetrap" |
| 3 | **Selling** | Customers won't buy enough unless **pushed** | Unsought goods; excess capacity; "sell what you make" |
| 4 | **Marketing** | Find needs and satisfy them **better than competitors** | Outside-in; customer satisfaction |
| 5 | **Societal** | Satisfy customers **and** society's long-term **well-being** | Balance company profit, consumer wants and society |
| 6 | **Holistic** | **Everything matters** (Kotler) | 4 pillars **RIIP**: Relationship, Integrated, Internal, Performance |

**Marketing concept: 5 steps**

1. Identify the target market.
2. Understand its needs and wants.
3. Develop products to satisfy them.
4. Satisfy them better than competitors.
5. Do it at a profit.

**⭐ Classic table: selling vs marketing concept**

| Factor | Selling concept | Marketing concept |
|---|---|---|
| Starting point | Factory | Market |
| Focus | Existing products | Customer needs |
| Means | Selling and promoting | Integrated marketing |
| Ends | Profit through **sales volume** | Profit through **customer satisfaction** |

**Marketing myopia:** focusing on the **product** instead of the **need** it serves. A railway company is really in the **transport** business, not just the train business.

**Economies of scale (5):** purchasing, managerial, financial, marketing, technological.

## Week 03 — Environmental Scanning & Marketing Planning

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** ஒரு நிறுவனத்தைச் சுற்றி **மூன்று அடுக்குச் சக்திகள்** உள்ளன:

- **Internal:** நிறுவனத்துக்கு உள்ளே.
- **Micro:** நேரடியாகத் தொடர்புடையவர்கள்.
- **Macro:** பெரிய சமூக சக்திகள்.

இவற்றைத் தொடர்ந்து **scan** செய்த பிறகுதான் plan போட வேண்டும். உதாரணம்: 2019 Easter attacks-க்குப் பிறகு tourism பெரிதும் பாதிக்கப்பட்டது.

| Layer | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Internal** | **5 Ms**: Men, Money, Machinery, Material, Market + the **McKinsey 7S** model |
| **Micro (task)** | Company, **suppliers**, **marketing intermediaries**, **customers**, **competitors**, **publics**: they affect the firm directly |
| **Macro** | **DENTPC**: Demographic, Economic, Natural, Technological, Political, Cultural |

**Micro details:**

- **Customer markets (5):** consumer, business, reseller, government, international.
- **Competitor roles:** leader, challenger, follower, nicher.
- **Publics (7):** financial, media, government, citizen-action, local, general, internal.

**Macro: one line each**

| Force | Key idea |
|---|---|
| Demographic | People make up markets: age, family, location, education, diversity |
| Economic | Purchasing power: income, inflation, interest, spending patterns |
| Natural | Raw-material shortages, pollution, climate, disasters |
| Technological | Fast change creates opportunities **and** threats |
| Political | Laws, government agencies, pressure groups |
| Cultural | Values, beliefs, lifestyles |

**Responding to the environment:**

- **Reactive:** adapt after the change happens.
- **Proactive:** influence the change through lobbying, PR, lawsuits or contracts.

**MIS:** people, equipment and procedures that give **timely, accurate information** to decision makers.

## Week 04 — STP: Segmentation, Targeting, Differentiation, Positioning

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** எல்லோருக்கும் விற்க முடியாது.

1. Market-ஐக் **குழுக்களாகப் பிரி** (segment).
2. சிறந்த குழுவைத் **தேர்ந்தெடு** (target).
3. போட்டியாளர்களிலிருந்து **வேறுபடுத்து** (differentiate).
4. வாடிக்கையாளர் **மனதில் ஒரு இடம்** பிடி (position).

**4 segmentation bases:**

| Base | Examples |
|---|---|
| Geographic | Country, province, city, climate |
| Demographic | Age, gender, income, family size, education |
| Psychographic | Lifestyle, personality, social class |
| Behavioural | **Occasion, usage rate, loyalty, benefits sought** |

**Why segment:**

- Meet needs more precisely.
- Higher sales and profit.
- Segment leadership.
- Customer retention.
- Focused communication.

**Effective segments must be MASDA:**

- **M**easurable
- **A**ccessible
- **S**ubstantial
- **D**ifferentiable
- **A**ctionable

**Targeting: evaluate 3 things**

1. Segment **size and growth**.
2. Segment **structural attractiveness**, using **Porter's 5 forces**: rivalry, threat of new entrants, threat of substitutes, supplier power, buyer power.
3. Company **objectives and resources**.

**5 target-market patterns:**

1. Single-segment concentration
2. Selective specialisation
3. Product specialisation
4. Market specialisation
5. Full market coverage

Full market coverage can be done in two ways:

- **Undifferentiated (mass):** one offer for everyone.
- **Differentiated:** a different offer for each segment.

**Differentiation:** price, performance and reliability, location and service.

**Positioning:** the place the brand occupies in customers' minds **relative to competitors**.

- **Perceptual map:** 2 axes. Find the **gaps**.
- **Steps:** identify possible competitive advantages → choose the right ones → communicate and deliver them.
- **Coca-Cola example:** emotional happiness ("Open Happiness"), universal appeal, consistent red branding.

## Week 05 — Consumer Buyer Behaviour

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** வாடிக்கையாளரின் மனம் ஒரு **"black box"**. உள்ளே 4P, environment போன்ற stimuli போகின்றன. வெளியே "எந்த brand, எப்போது, எவ்வளவு" என்ற முடிவு வருகிறது. அந்த box-க்குள் இருப்பவை இரண்டு: **வாங்குபவரின் குணங்கள்** (4 factor groups), **முடிவெடுக்கும் process** (5 stages).

**⭐ Black box model (draw it):**

| Stimuli | → Buyer's black box | → Responses |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing: **4 Ps** | Buyer characteristics | Product choice, brand choice |
| Other: economic, technological, political, cultural | Buyer decision process | Dealer choice, timing, amount |

**4 factor groups:**

| Group | Factors |
|---|---|
| **Cultural** | Culture (most fundamental), subculture, social class |
| **Social** | Reference groups, family, roles and status |
| **Personal** | Age and life-cycle stage, occupation and economic situation, lifestyle (AIO), personality and self-concept |
| **Psychological** | Motivation (Maslow, Herzberg, McClelland), learning, beliefs and attitudes, perception |

**Reference groups:**

- Membership groups: **primary** (family, friends) and **secondary** (clubs).
- Non-membership groups: **aspirational** (groups you want to join) and **dissociative** (groups you avoid).

**Buying roles:** initiator, influencer, decider, buyer, user, discarder.

**5-stage decision process:**

1. **Need recognition**
2. **Information search**: personal, commercial, public and experiential sources
3. **Evaluation of alternatives**
4. **Purchase decision**
5. **Post-purchase behaviour**: watch for **cognitive dissonance**, the doubt after buying

**⭐ 4 buying behaviours (2×2 grid):**

| | High involvement | Low involvement |
|---|---|---|
| **Big brand differences** | **Complex** (car, laptop) | **Variety-seeking** (biscuits) |
| **Few brand differences** | **Dissonance-reducing** (carpet, tiles) | **Habitual** (salt, sugar) |

## Week 06 — Business Markets & Business Buyer Behaviour

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** Business market என்பது **நிறுவனங்கள் வாங்குவது**. அவை வாங்குவது மற்ற பொருட்களைத் தயாரிக்க அல்லது விற்க. Consumer market-இலிருந்து இது எப்படி வேறுபடுகிறது:

- வாங்குபவர்கள் **குறைவு**, ஆனால் அளவு **பெரியது**.
- முடிவுகளில் **பலர்** சம்பந்தப்படுவார்கள்.
- Demand **derived** ஆனது: consumer demand-இலிருந்து வருகிறது.

உதாரணம்: ஒரு shoe consumer-ஐ அடையும் வரை hide dealer, tanner, manufacturer, wholesaler, retailer என்று பல B2B விற்பனைகள் நடக்கின்றன.

**B2B differences:**

- Fewer, larger buyers
- Close supplier–customer relationships
- Professional purchasing
- Many buying influences
- Multiple sales calls
- **Derived, inelastic, fluctuating demand**
- Geographically concentrated buyers
- Direct purchasing

**Acceleration effect:** a small change in consumer demand can cause a **large** change in purchases of machines and other capital goods.

**3 buying situations:**

| Situation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Straight rebuy** | Reorder the same item routinely |
| **Modified rebuy** | Change the specs, price or supplier |
| **New task** | First-time purchase: most people involved and most information needed |

**Systems selling:** a turnkey **total solution**. Systems contracting is one form, e.g. Shell managing a customer's MRO inventory.

**Buying centre (7 roles):** Initiators, Users, Influencers, Deciders, Approvers, Buyers, Gatekeepers.

**Procurement orientation:** buying (price) → procurement (quality + cost) → supply-chain management (strategic).

**Kraljic's 4 product types:** routine, leverage, strategic, bottleneck.

**8 buy phases (Robinson et al.):**

1. Problem recognition
2. General need description
3. Product specification
4. Supplier search
5. Proposal solicitation
6. Supplier selection
7. Order-routine specification
8. Performance review

## Week 07 — Marketing Information & Research

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** முடிவுகள் **உணர்வை (gut feel)** வைத்து அல்ல, **facts**-ஐ வைத்து எடுக்கப்பட வேண்டும். அந்தத் தகவல்கள் **MIS** மூலம் கிடைக்கின்றன. Research **4 படிகளில்** நடக்கிறது.

Shoe salesmen கதையை நினைவில் வையுங்கள். ஒருவர் "யாரும் shoe அணிவதில்லை, market இல்லை" என்றார். இன்னொருவர் "யாரும் இன்னும் அணியவில்லை, பெரிய market" என்றார். ஒரே data, இரண்டு எதிரெதிர் முடிவுகள். அதனால்தான் **evidence** தேவை.

**3 data types:** internal data, competitive marketing intelligence, marketing research.

**MIS components:**

- Internal records
- Marketing intelligence
- Marketing research
- Marketing decision support system (MDSS)

**Intelligence scanning:** undirected viewing, conditioned viewing, informal search, formal search.

**Research suppliers:** syndicated, custom, specialty-line.

**4 research types:**

| Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Exploratory** | Gather preliminary insight and define the problem |
| **Descriptive** | Who, what, how many (market size, profiles) |
| **Causal** | Test cause and effect |
| **Predictive** | Forecast the future |

**Research methods:**

- **Qualitative:** focus groups (6–12 people), depth interviews, photo-ethnography.
- **Quantitative surveys:** telephone, personal, web, hybrid.

**⭐ Research process (4 steps):**

1. **Define** the problem and research objectives.
2. **Develop** the research plan and **collect** the information. Plan decisions: data sources, research approach, research instrument, sampling plan, contact method.
3. **Analyse** the information.
4. **Present** the findings.

**Primary vs secondary data:**

- **Primary:** collected new for this study.
- **Secondary:** already exists.

**Why research fails:**

- Narrow view of research
- Weak researchers
- Poor problem framing (the **New Coke** case)
- Slow or late results
- Clash of styles between researchers and managers

## Week 08 — Digital Marketing

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** Digital marketing என்பது **digital channels** மூலம் செய்யும் marketing: search, social, display, mobile, video. இதன் பலம் **அளக்க முடிவது**. ஆனால் **ஒவ்வொரு எண்ணும் KPI ஆகாது**. Business வெற்றியுடன் நேரடியாகத் தொடர்புடைய எண்கள் மட்டுமே KPI.

**4 objectives:**

1. Direct sales
2. Lead generation
3. Lower customer-acquisition cost
4. Engagement

**Website 5 musts:**

1. Fast
2. Secure (HTTPS)
3. Mobile-friendly
4. Clean UX
5. SEO

**Strategy components:**

| Component | Key point |
|---|---|
| **SEO** | On-page + off-page (backlinks). Slow but long-lasting |
| **Social media** | Content, community, paid reach |
| **PPC** | Pay per click, e.g. Google Ads |
| **Remarketing** | Re-engage people who visited before (within permission limits) |
| **Affiliate** | Commission per agreed outcome |
| **Email** | Opt-in lists. Land in the inbox, not spam. Strong subject lines |

**Kaushik measurement model:** Objectives → Goals → KPIs → Targets → Segments.

**Metric vs KPI:**

- **Metric:** any number.
- **KPI:** a number tied to business success, e.g. sales, ROMI, cost per lead, conversion rate.
- **Hotel example:** online booking revenue, cost per booking and ROMI are KPIs. Visits and bounce rate are only supporting metrics.

## Week 09 — New Product Development & PLC

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** புதிய product **8 படிகளில்** பிறக்கிறது. Market-இல் வந்த பிறகு அது **4 கட்ட வாழ்க்கையை (PLC)** வாழ்கிறது. ஒவ்வொரு கட்டத்துக்கும் **வேறு 4P strategy** தேவை. கீழே உள்ள table தான் **gold**: exam-இல் அடிக்கடி வருகிறது.

**6 types of new products:**

1. Original (new to the world)
2. Improvements
3. Modifications
4. New brands
5. Repositioned products
6. Cost reductions

**Why products fail:**

- Research ignored
- Market over-estimated
- Wrong target
- Poor design or positioning
- Wrong price
- Weak advertising
- High costs
- Competitor reaction

**⭐ 8 NPD stages:**

1. Idea generation
2. Idea screening
3. Concept development and testing
4. Marketing strategy development
5. Business analysis
6. Product development
7. Test marketing
8. Commercialisation: decide **when, where, to whom, how**

**Idea vs concept vs image:**

- **Idea:** a possible product.
- **Concept:** a detailed version of the idea in consumer terms.
- **Image:** how consumers picture the product.

**Sequential vs simultaneous development:**

- **Sequential:** one stage after another.
- **Simultaneous:** cross-functional teams work on overlapping stages, which is faster.

**PLC basics:**

- Stages: Introduction → Growth → Maturity → Decline.
- Profit peaks in **growth and maturity**.
- **Shapes:** style (cyclic), fashion (rises and falls slowly), fad (rises fast, falls fast).
- **Customers by stage:** innovators → early adopters → middle majority → laggards.

**⭐ Strategies by PLC stage:**

| | Introduction | Growth | Maturity | Decline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Product** | Basic product | Extensions, service, warranty | Diversify brands and models | Phase out weak items |
| **Price** | Cost-plus | Penetration | Match or beat competitors | Cut price |
| **Distribution** | Selective | Intensive | More intensive | Selective; drop unprofitable outlets |
| **Advertising** | Awareness among early adopters and dealers | Mass-market awareness | Stress brand differences | Reduce to keep hard-core loyals |
| **Sales promotion** | Heavy, to get trial | Reduce | Increase, for brand switching | Minimum |

**PLC objectives:** Introduction = create awareness and trial · Growth = maximise market share · Maturity = maximise profit while defending share · Decline = reduce expenditure and milk the brand.

**Can the PLC be used in a turbulent environment?** Yes, as a **framework**. But stages and their lengths become unpredictable, so combine it with market data.

## Week 10 — Product & Branding Strategies

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** Product-க்கு **3 அடுக்குகள்** உள்ளன:

- **Core:** என்ன நன்மை தருகிறது.
- **Actual:** design, brand, features.
- **Augmented:** warranty, service.

Brand என்பது **பெயர் மற்றும் சின்னம்**. அது தரும் **மதிப்புதான் brand equity**.

**3 product levels (phone example):**

- **Core:** communication.
- **Actual:** design, brand, features, quality, packaging.
- **Augmented:** warranty, after-sales service, delivery.

**Services are IIVP:** Intangible, Inseparable, Variable, Perishable.

**Consumer products:**

| Type | Example | Marketing |
|---|---|---|
| **Convenience** (staple, impulse, emergency) | Soap, chocolate, umbrella in the rain | Low price, **intensive** distribution |
| **Shopping** | Clothes, furniture | Compare before buying; selective distribution |
| **Specialty** | Rolex | Strong loyalty; exclusive distribution |
| **Unsought** | Insurance | Hard selling, advertising |

**Product mix:**

- **Width:** number of product lines.
- **Length:** total number of items.
- **Depth:** variants of each item.
- **Consistency:** how closely related the lines are.

**Line decisions:**

- Analysis, length
- **Stretching:** up, down or both ways
- **Filling:** add items within the current range
- Modernisation, pruning

**Brand (AMA):** a name, term, sign, symbol or design that **identifies** a seller's goods and **differentiates** them from competitors'.

**6 brand meanings (Mercedes example):** attributes, benefits, values, culture, personality, user.

**Aaker's 5 levels of brand loyalty** (lowest → highest):

1. Price-switcher
2. Satisfied / habitual buyer
3. Satisfied buyer with switching costs
4. Likes the brand, treats it as a friend
5. Devoted, committed buyer

**Branding decisions:**

- **Sponsor:** manufacturer brand, private brand or licensed brand.
- **Name strategy:**
    - Individual names
    - Blanket family name (J&J)
    - Separate family names
    - Company + individual name (Nestlé Maggi)
- **Brand strategy:**

| Strategy | Product category | Brand name |
|---|---|---|
| Line extension | Existing | Existing |
| Brand extension | **New** | Existing |
| Multibrands | Existing | **New** |
| New brands | **New** | **New** |

- **Co-brands:** two brands on one product.

**Repositioning example:** 7 Up → "the Uncola".

**A good brand name:**

- Suggests benefits (Samaposha)
- Suggests qualities (Perlwhite)
- Easy to pronounce and remember (Rin)
- Distinctive (Kodak)
- No bad meaning in other languages

**Packaging:**

- **Levels:** primary, secondary, shipping.
- **Tests:** engineering, visual, dealer, consumer.

## Week 11 — Pricing Strategies

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** 4P-இல் **வருமானம் தருவது price மட்டுமே**. மற்ற மூன்றும் செலவுகள். Price-ஐப் பாதிப்பவை இரண்டு வகை: **internal** factors, **external** factors. Price அமைப்பது **6 படிகளில்** நடக்கிறது.

3% margin உள்ள ஒரு business-இல் **1% விலை உயர்வு லாபத்தை சுமார் 33% கூட்டும்**. கணக்கு: sales Rs 100-இல் லாபம் Rs 3. விலை 1% உயர்ந்தால் sales Rs 101, செலவு அதே, லாபம் Rs 4. அதாவது +33%.

**Factors:**

- **Internal:** objectives, marketing mix, costs, organisation, differentiation.
- **External:** demand, competition, economy and government, customers' willingness to pay, channels.

**⭐ 6-step pricing process:**

1. Select the pricing objective
2. Determine demand
3. Estimate costs
4. Analyse competitors
5. Select a pricing method
6. Select the final price

**Objectives:** survival, maximum current profit, market skimming, market share, quality leadership.

**Elasticity** = % change in quantity ÷ % change in price.

- **Elastic:** demand is sensitive to price.
- **Inelastic:** demand is not sensitive to price.

**Cost concepts:** fixed and variable costs, ABC, target costing.

**Pricing methods (6):**

1. Cost-plus (markup)
2. Target-return
3. Good-value
4. Value-added
5. Going-rate
6. Sealed-bid (auction)

**New-product pricing:**

| | Skimming | Penetration |
|---|---|---|
| Price | **High** at launch, lowered over time | **Low** at launch |
| Goal | Skim the most from early buyers | Win market share fast |
| Example | iPhone | New low-cost telecom package |

**7 price adjustments:**

1. Discounts and allowances
2. Segmented pricing
3. Psychological pricing (Rs 999)
4. Promotional pricing
5. Geographical pricing: FOB-origin, uniform-delivered, zone, basing-point, freight-absorption
6. Dynamic pricing
7. International pricing

**Price changes:**

- **Price cuts:** used for excess capacity or to gain share. Risk of a price war. Can **damage a luxury image** (Rolex).
- **Price increases:** usually because of inflation (cost push).

## Week 12 — Promotion & IMC

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** வாடிக்கையாளர் TV, Facebook, SMS, கடை என்று பல இடங்களில் ஒரு brand-ஐப் பார்க்கிறார். எல்லா இடத்திலும் **ஒரே செய்தி, ஒரே வாக்குறுதி** இருக்க வேண்டும். இதுவே **IMC**. Promotion-க்கு **5 கருவிகள்** உள்ளன.

**IMC:** carefully **coordinating all promotion tools** to deliver a **clear, consistent and compelling** message.

**Why IMC is needed now:**

- Consumers are changing.
- Marketing strategies are changing.
- New technology.

**6 promotion objectives:**

1. Stimulate demand
2. Inform
3. Persuade
4. Launch a new product
5. Face competition
6. Build image

**⭐ Promotion mix (Sri Lankan telecom example):**

| Tool | Telecom example |
|---|---|
| Advertising | TV and digital ads (Dialog, Mobitel) |
| Personal selling | Staff in retail outlets |
| Sales promotion | Reload and data bonuses |
| Public relations | Cricket and event sponsorships |
| Direct marketing | SMS offers |

**Ad types:**

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Informative | eZ Cash |
| Persuasive | |
| Comparison | Hutch vs Airtel |
| Reminder | Coke at cricket matches |

**Ad strategy:**

- Decide based on product qualities, the state of the market, goals and methodology.
- Then two decisions: the **message** and the **media**.

**Message strategy:**

- **Appeals:** rational, emotional, moral.
- **Structure:**
    - Draw a conclusion, or leave it to the audience?
    - Strongest argument first or last?
    - One-sided or two-sided?
- **Format:** headline, copy, illustration, colour.

**PR tools:** media relations, advertorials, social media, newsletters, events, sponsorships.

**Sales promotion:** short-term incentives that create limited-time pressure.

- **Consumer promotions:** coupons, samples, contests.
- **Trade promotions:** discounts and allowances for dealers.

**⭐ Push vs pull:**

| | Push | Pull |
|---|---|---|
| How | Sales force + trade promotion push the product **through** the channel | Advertising + consumer promotion **pull** customers to ask for it |
| Example | Nokia, Mars | Intel Inside |

**Social responsibility:** recyclable packs, cause campaigns, charity. **Avoid greenwashing.**

## Week 13 — Place: Channels, Logistics & Supply Chain

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** Product **தயாரிப்பாளரிடமிருந்து வாடிக்கையாளரை அடையும் பாதை** தான் place.

- **Channel levels:** இடையில் எத்தனை பேர் இருக்கிறார்கள்.
- **Conflict:** அவர்களுக்குள் சண்டை.
- **VMS:** அவர்களை ஒரே system ஆக இணைப்பது.

**Marketing channel:** a set of **interdependent organisations** that make a product available to consumers.

**What channel members do:**

- Negotiation
- Holding inventory
- Communication and promotion
- Market information
- Risk taking
- Financing
- Physical distribution

**Why use intermediaries:**

- The producer lacks money or skills.
- Direct marketing isn't feasible.
- Intermediaries are more efficient.

**⭐ Channel levels:**

| Level | Path |
|---|---|
| 0 (direct) | Producer → Consumer |
| 1 | Producer → **Retailer** → Consumer |
| 2 | Producer → **Wholesaler** → Retailer → Consumer |
| 3 | Producer → **Agent** → Wholesaler → Retailer → Consumer |

**Conflict:**

- **Horizontal:** between firms at the **same** level.
- **Vertical:** between **different** levels. More common.

**Conventional channel vs VMS:**

- **Conventional:** independent members, each maximising its own profit.
- **VMS:** members act as **one unified system**.

**3 VMS types:**

| Type | How it is held together | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate | Ownership | Apple stores |
| Contractual | Contract or franchise | McDonald's |
| Administered | Power of one big member | Walmart |

**Horizontal marketing system:** firms at the same level join forces, e.g. a bank ATM inside a supermarket.

**Channel design (4 steps):**

1. **Analyse consumer needs** using the 5 service outputs:
    - Lot size
    - Waiting time
    - Spatial convenience
    - Product variety
    - Service backup
2. **Set channel objectives.**
3. **Identify the major alternatives:**
    - Types of intermediaries
    - Number of intermediaries: **intensive** (convenience goods), **selective** (shopping goods), **exclusive** (luxury)
    - Responsibilities of channel members
4. **Evaluate the alternatives** on economic, control and adaptive criteria.

**Logistics:**

- **Types:** outbound, inbound, reverse.
- **Goal:** deliver the target service level at the lowest cost.
- **Functions:**
    - Warehousing
    - Inventory management
    - Transportation (rail, truck, water, pipeline, air)
    - Order processing
    - Packaging
    - Logistics information management

**Answer frame: luxury fashion brand.** Exclusive distribution, few channel levels (often a corporate VMS with its own boutiques), high service backup, and tight control over price and image.

## Week 14 — The Marketing Plan

**தமிழ் சுருக்கம்:** Marketing plan என்பது ஒரு **business document**. அது "எங்கே போகிறோம், எப்படிப் போகிறோம், எவ்வளவு செலவாகும், எப்படி அளப்போம்" என்று சொல்கிறது. இந்தப் பாடம் **முந்தைய எல்லா வாரங்களையும் ஒன்றாக இணைக்கிறது**. Railway ஒப்புமை: marketing என்பது மற்ற departments-ஐ **இழுக்கும் engine**.

**Marketing plan:** a business document that sets out the **strategy, costs, goals and actions** for a set period.

**Importance:**

- Reduces uncertainty
- Coordination
- Effective implementation
- Balances resources
- Market enlargement
- Basis for appraisal
- SWOT awareness

**⭐ Components (in order):**

1. **Executive summary**: the "narrator". Gives investors the whole picture.
2. **Company description**
3. **Macro-environment analysis**: DENTPC
4. **Current marketing situation**: customers, competitors, suppliers, channels
5. **SWOT**: strengths and weaknesses are internal; opportunities and threats come from the environment
6. **Objectives**
7. **Marketing strategy**: segmentation → targeting → positioning → 4 Ps
8. **Action programmes**: what, when, who, how much
9. **Budget**: a projected profit and loss statement
10. **Controls**: monitor progress and return on marketing investment

**How environmental scanning supports the plan:** the macro forces feed the **opportunities and threats** in the SWOT, and these shape the objectives.

**Telecom plan activity:**

1. Pick a mobile operator.
2. Invent a product, e.g. a **student data bundle** with zero-rated study apps.
3. Walk it through all 10 components.
