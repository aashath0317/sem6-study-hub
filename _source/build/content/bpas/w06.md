## The statement of financial position (SFP)

:::key Definition
The **statement of financial position** (balance sheet) shows **a business's wealth at a particular date**: its **assets, liabilities and capital**.
:::

| Element | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Non-current assets** | Used for **more than one year**, not for resale | Land, buildings, machinery, motor vehicles, furniture (at **cost less accumulated depreciation**) |
| **Current assets** | Turned into cash **within 12 months** or the operating cycle | **Inventory (closing stock), trade receivables (debtors), prepayments, bank, cash** |
| **Current liabilities** | Due **within 12 months** | **Trade payables (creditors), accruals, bank overdraft**, the current part of loans |
| **Non-current liabilities** | Due **after 12 months** | Long-term bank loans |
| **Capital (owner's equity)** | The owner's claim | Opening capital + net profit (+ capital introduced) − drawings |

**Format (sole trader):**

| Statement of financial position as at 31 March 20xx | Rs | Rs |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Non-current assets** (cost − accumulated depreciation) | | xxx |
| **Current assets**: inventory, receivables, prepayments, bank, cash | xxx | |
| **Total assets** | | **xxxx** |
| **Capital**: opening + net profit − drawings | | xxx |
| **Non-current liabilities**: long-term loan | | xxx |
| **Current liabilities**: payables, accruals, overdraft | | xxx |
| **Total capital and liabilities** | | **xxxx** |

## Adjustment 1: closing inventory

- **Why it's an adjustment:** goods bought are recorded as an **expense** (Purchases a/c), so the **value of unsold stock isn't in the books**.
- At year end, it's **physically counted** and valued at the **lower of cost and net realisable value** (LKAS 2, Week 8).

:::formula
**Closing stock A/C  Dr**  /  **Cost of sales (Trading A/C)  Cr**
:::

**Effects:**

1. It **reduces cost of sales**, which **increases gross profit**.
2. It appears as a **current asset** on the SFP.

**This year's closing stock is next year's opening stock.**

## LKAS 16 — Property, Plant & Equipment

**Objective:** prescribe the accounting for PPE: **recognition**, **carrying amounts**, **depreciation** and **impairment**.

:::key Definition of PPE
**Tangible** items that are:

1. **held for use** in production or supply of goods or services, **for rental to others**, or for **administrative purposes**, **and**
2. **expected to be used for more than one period**.
:::

**Recognition:** it's **probable** that future economic benefits will flow to the entity, **and** the cost can be **measured reliably**.

### Initial measurement: at cost
Cost = **everything needed to bring the asset to working condition for its intended use**.

|---|---:|
| Purchase price | xxxx |
| Less: trade discounts | (xxx) |
| Less: subsidies | (xxx) |
| Carriage inwards (delivery and handling) | xxxx |
| Direct and **non-refundable** taxes / import duties | xxxx |
| **Installation** charges | xxxx |
| **Test-run** expenses | xxxx |
| Site preparation, professional fees (architects, engineers) | xxxx |
| Estimated **dismantling and site-restoration** cost | xxxx |
| **Total cost** | **xxxx** |

:::warn Not part of cost
**Refundable** taxes (e.g. VAT that can be claimed back), **repairs and maintenance** (revenue expenditure, Week 4), training, and general overheads.
:::

### Subsequent measurement: two models

:::grid
**Cost model**

Carried at **cost − accumulated depreciation − impairment**
+++
**Revaluation model**

Carried at **fair value at the revaluation date − later depreciation and impairment**, if fair value can be **measured reliably** (gains go to OCI, Week 5)
:::

### Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Depreciable amount** | **Cost − residual value** |
| **Residual value** | The **scrap value** at the end of the useful life |
| **Useful life** | The period over which the asset is expected to be used |
| **Depreciation** | **Systematic allocation** of the depreciable amount **over the useful life** |
| **Carrying amount (NBV)** | Cost − accumulated depreciation − impairment |

**All PPE is depreciated except land.**

## Adjustment 2: depreciation

:::formula
**Depreciation A/C  Dr**  /  **Provision for (accumulated) depreciation A/C  Cr**
:::

- The **expense** goes to the income statement.
- The **provision** is deducted from cost on the SFP.

| Method | Formula | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Straight-line** | **(Cost − Residual value) ÷ Useful life** | The **same charge every year** |
| **Reducing balance** | Charge = **rate × NBV at the start of the year**, where **rate = 1 − ⁿ√(S/C)** × 100% | **Higher early**, decreasing later |

*(n = useful life, S = residual value, C = cost.)*

## Disposal of PPE

An asset is **derecognised** (removed from the SFP) when it's **disposed of**, or **withdrawn from use** with no further benefits expected. The **gain or loss = proceeds − carrying amount**, recognised in **profit or loss**.

**Steps using a disposal account:**

1. **Remove the cost:** Dr **Disposal** / Cr **Asset (cost)**
2. **Remove the accumulated depreciation:** Dr **Provision for depreciation** / Cr **Disposal**
3. **Record the proceeds:** Dr **Cash / Debtor** / Cr **Disposal**
4. **Balancing figure:** a **credit balance** on the disposal account is a **profit**; a **debit balance** is a **loss**. Transfer it to the income statement.

### LKAS 16 disclosures (for each class of PPE)
- **Measurement basis** for the carrying amount
- **Depreciation method(s)**
- **Useful lives or depreciation rates**
- **Gross carrying amount and accumulated depreciation and impairment**
- **A reconciliation** of the carrying amount from start to end of the period

:::exam
Expect:

- **Prepare the SFP** (classify current vs non-current correctly; capital + profit − drawings)
- **The closing-stock journal** and its effect
- **The LKAS 16 definition and recognition** of PPE
- **Calculating cost** (include installation and test runs; exclude refundable taxes and trade discounts)
- **Cost vs revaluation model**
- **Straight-line and reducing-balance calculations** (use 1 − ⁿ√(S/C))
- **The disposal account** and the profit or loss
:::

### Cost of PPE (LKAS 16)

:::key What goes into cost
**Include:** purchase price **less trade discounts**, import duties and **non-refundable taxes**, delivery, installation, and professional (legal) fees.

**Exclude:** **recoverable VAT**, insurance for the year (an expense), and training.
:::

### Disposals

:::example Q8 to Q11: Profit or loss on disposal
| Q | Cost | Accumulated depreciation at disposal | NBV | Proceeds | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 800,000 | 300,000 (sold 1 Jan, no charge for 2019) | 500,000 | 600,000 | **Profit 100,000** |
| 9 | 500,000 | 200,000 + 2018 charge 50,000 = 250,000 | 250,000 | 400,000 | **Profit 150,000** |
| 10 | 600,000 | 2 years × 120,000 + 6 months 60,000 = 300,000 | 300,000 | 500,000 | **Profit 200,000** |
| 11 | 500,000 | 9 months 56,250 + 6 months 37,500 = 93,750 | 406,250 | 300,000 | **Loss 106,250** |

**Depreciation for the year:**

- **Q10:** sold machine 60,000 + the rest (400,000 × 20%) 80,000 = **140,000**
- **Q11:** sold furniture 37,500 + the rest (300,000 × 15%) 45,000 = **82,500**

Q10 and Q11 assume straight line on cost, apportioned by month.
:::

:::tip Disposal account
**Dr** Disposal with the **cost**. **Cr** Disposal with the **accumulated depreciation** and the **proceeds**. The balancing figure is the **profit** (credit side short) or **loss** (debit side short).
:::

## Quick revision sheet

| Concept | Remember it as |
|---|---|
| SFP | Wealth at a date: assets = capital + liabilities |
| Current assets | Inventory, receivables, prepayments, bank, cash |
| Current liabilities | Payables, accruals, overdraft |
| Capital | Opening + profit − drawings |
| Closing stock | Lower of cost and NRV; Dr Closing stock / Cr Trading; lowers COS, raises GP |
| PPE | Tangible, used in operations, more than one period |
| Recognition | Probable benefits + reliable cost |
| Cost | Price − discounts − subsidies + carriage + non-refundable taxes + installation + test run + dismantling |
| Models | Cost vs revaluation |
| Depreciable amount | Cost − residual value |
| SL | (C − S) ÷ n |
| RB | Rate = 1 − ⁿ√(S/C), applied to NBV |
| Land | Not depreciated |
| Disposal | Proceeds − carrying amount = gain or loss |
