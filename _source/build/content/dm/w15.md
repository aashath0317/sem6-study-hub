## Overview

Earlier lessons covered the **principles and methods** of data mining. This lesson looks at **application domains** and how **customised data mining tools** should be built for each.

| Domain | Why data mining fits | Typical uses |
|---|---|---|
| **Financial** | Data is **complete, reliable and high-quality** | Data warehouses, **loan payment prediction**, credit policy, **money-laundering detection** |
| **Retail** | **Vast** data on sales, customers, transport and service, growing with **e-commerce** | Multidimensional analysis, **sales-campaign effectiveness**, customer retention |
| **Telecommunication** | Highly **competitive**, multidimensional call data | Traffic analysis, **fraud detection**, association and sequential patterns |
| **Biological** | **Explosive growth** of genomics and proteomics | Data integration, **sequence alignment** (BLAST, FASTA), protein networks |

## Financial data analysis

Banks and financial institutions offer **banking services** (checking and savings accounts), **credit** (business, mortgage and automobile loans), **investment services** (mutual funds), and sometimes insurance and stock investment.

:::key Why finance suits data mining
Financial data is usually **relatively complete, reliable and of high quality**, which makes **systematic** analysis and mining easier.
:::

| Case | What data mining does |
|---|---|
| **Data warehouses for multidimensional analysis** | Build warehouses for banking data and analyse its **general properties** multidimensionally |
| **Loan payment prediction and credit policy** | **Attribute selection** and **attribute relevance ranking** find the **critical factors** that influence loan repayment and credit rating, and remove irrelevant ones |
| **Money laundering and financial crime** | **Integrate multiple databases** (bank transactions, federal/state crime history), then use analysis tools to spot **unusual patterns**, e.g. **large cash flows** at certain periods by certain groups of customers |

## Retail industry

Retail collects **vast amounts of data** on sales, customer shopping history, goods transportation, consumption and service. It keeps growing because of **e-commerce**; some businesses such as **Amazon** exist **only online**, with no physical store.

**What retail data mining helps with:**

- Identify customer **buying behaviour**
- Discover **shopping patterns and trends**
- Improve **customer service** quality
- Achieve better customer **retention and satisfaction**
- Enhance **goods consumption ratios**
- Design more effective **transportation and distribution** policies
- **Reduce** the cost of business

| Case | What data mining does |
|---|---|
| **Data warehouses** | Retail data spans sales, customers, employees, transport, consumption and services, so there are **many ways** to design the warehouse |
| **Multidimensional analysis** of sales, customers, products, **time and region** | Timely information on needs, sales, trends and fashions; **data cubes** and visualisation tools |
| **Sales-campaign effectiveness** | Compare **sales and transactions** containing the promoted items **during** the campaign vs **before or after** it |

## Telecommunication industry

Telecoms grew from local and long-distance calls to **fax, pager, cellular phone, internet messenger, images, e-mail and web data**. Telecom, computer networks and the internet are **integrating**, and **deregulation** makes the market **fast-expanding and highly competitive**.

Data mining helps to **understand the business**, find **telecom patterns**, **catch fraud**, **use resources better** and **improve service quality**.

| Scenario | What data mining does |
|---|---|
| **Multidimensional analysis** | Call data is naturally multidimensional: **calling time, duration, caller location, callee location, call type**. Compare **traffic, workload, resource usage, user-group behaviour and profit** |
| **Fraudulent and unusual patterns** | Fraud costs **millions of dollars a year** (see below) |
| **Association and sequential patterns** | Find usage patterns for services **by customer group, month and time of day** to promote services |

:::key Three fraud tasks
1. Identify **potentially fraudulent users** and their **atypical usage patterns**
2. Detect attempts to gain **fraudulent entry to customer accounts**
3. Discover **unusual patterns** needing attention, e.g. **busy-hour frustrated call attempts**, **switch and route congestion**, and **periodic calls from badly programmed auto-dial equipment** (like fax machines)
:::

## Biological data analysis

The past decade saw **explosive growth** in **genomics, proteomics, functional genomics and biomedical research**: comparing the genomes of humans and other species, genetic networks, protein pathways, new drugs and **cancer therapies**. Biological data mining is now a core part of **bioinformatics**.

| Topic | Key points |
|---|---|
| **Semantic integration** of genomic and proteomic databases | Data comes from **different labs and methods**: distributed, **heterogeneous** and varied. Integration, plus linking **research literature** to biological entities, enables cross-site analysis |
| **Alignment, indexing and comparison** of nucleotide/protein sequences | Tools such as **BLAST** and **FASTA**. Unlike normal sequential pattern mining, they must allow **gaps and mismatches** to handle **insertions, deletions and mutations** |
| **Structural patterns, genetic networks and protein pathways** | Proteins fold into **3-D structures** that interact by position and distance, forming complex networks. Find **structural patterns and regularities** |
