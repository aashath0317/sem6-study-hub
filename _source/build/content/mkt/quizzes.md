| Q | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **societal marketing** | Societal marketing balances company profit, customer wants and society's long-term well-being. |
| 2 | **sales** | Selling (sales) concept: customers will not buy enough unless the company sells and promotes aggressively. |
| 3 | **Supplier** | There is no “supplier concept”. The philosophies are production, product, selling, marketing, societal and holistic. |
| 4 | **Production concept** | Production concept: consumers prefer products that are widely available and cheap, so improve production and distribution efficiency. |
| 5 | **achieving long-term organizational goals is as important as satisfying customers** | Goal orientation: satisfy customers in a way that also achieves the firm's long-term goals (such as profit and survival). |
